# PSM Prep v2.2 - Acronym Knowledge Release

## Added
- Expanded Acronym Center from 26 to 75 book-located records.
- Unique `key` for every acronym record.
- Verification status and internal verification log.
- Book traceability fields: element, page, source and knowledge level.
- Conflict and confusion metadata, including HAZOP/HAZID, PRV/PSV, lower/upper limits, PFP/AFP and dual-meaning PFD.
- Proactive classification for hazard-analysis techniques only as `Derived from Book`.
- Random, sequential and weak-acronym challenge modes.
- 20, 30 and all-question challenge lengths.
- Wrong-acronym tracking in local storage.
- Mastered and Needs Review learning status.
- Same-topic distractor preference to reduce obviously incorrect options.

## Integrity rules
- Unsupported descriptions are not invented.
- Rejected acronyms remain outside the learner-facing center.
- LOPA is recorded as rejected because it was not found in the supplied book.
